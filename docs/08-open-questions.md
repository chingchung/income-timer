# 08 — Open questions

Decisions needed from you. Ordered by how much they change the build.

1. **Build environment.** Mac mini, hosted macOS builds, or park Phase 1 until
   later? This gates everything native.

2. **Headline number: net or gross?** The brief assumes net today, because that
   is what makes expenses bite. Gross is the purer gimmick and never depresses
   the user. Both exist; which one is the default on the widget?

3. **Unpaid lunch, default on or off?** Off gives a bigger, simpler number. On
   is more accurate. Currently specced as off.

4. **Hong Kong only, or global from day one?** Multi-currency is cheap. The
   holiday calendar, the language, and the celebrity list are not. Currently
   specced as HK-first, multi-currency, Traditional Chinese first.

5. **After-tax and MPF.** HK salaries tax is paid annually, not deducted
   monthly, so subtracting it makes the live number less recognisable as your
   pay. Show gross by default with an optional MPF deduction, or model tax fully?

6. **How dark is the tone?** The concept is a coping mechanism for a boring job
   and the copy can lean into that. There is a version of this app that is
   funny-bleak and a version that is neutral-utility. The funny one markets
   itself and ages less well.

7. **Celebrity list.** Which names? The sourcing rules in the landing page doc
   constrain this more than you might expect. A list of listed-company chief
   executives is defensible and slightly dry; a list of entertainers is fun and
   needs more care.

8. **Name.** "Income Timer" is descriptive and available as a repo, but it is
   not memorable and it does not hint at the expense half. Worth a pass before
   anything is published, since it affects the domain and the App Store listing.
